Each spell is written with its base difficulty in brackets, then its
properties. Difficulty, cost and boosting all work as [[spellcasting]]
and [[spell-properties]] describe.

## Cure / Cause Wounds (16)

- **Schools:** life force
- **Domains:** healing, or harm when reversed
- **Range:** touch
- **Minimum spirit:** 1

Restores hit point damage to the recipient. Cast as *Cause Wounds*, the
same spell instead inflicts damage.

## Force Bolt (2)

- **Schools:** energy
- **Domains:** magic
- **Range:** 10, needs an attack roll
- **Damage:** 4 (force)
- **Save:** none
- **Minimum spirit:** 0

Fires bolts of magical energy that deal force damage. Non-standard
boosts:

- **Extra bolts:** +4 difficulty per additional bolt.
- **Damage boost:** +1 damage per bolt for each +1 difficulty.
