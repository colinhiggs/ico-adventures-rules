These powers are open to any character. Each is written as *(power
source, skill, difficulty)*, and is resolved as [[using-powers]]
describes. Powers tied to a particular specialisation are not covered
here.

## Fast Melee 1

*(stamina, melee attack, difficulty {{ mechanics.fast_melee_1_difficulty }})*

Gain an extra melee attack, normally taken straight after your first.

Roll and modify the extra attack as normal and call the result `R`.
First compare `R` to the power's difficulty to settle whether the power
worked and what stamina it cost. If it worked, resolve the attack using
that same `R` against the target's [[hitting|targeting difficulty]].

## Fast Melee 2

*(stamina, melee attack, difficulty {{ mechanics.fast_melee_2_difficulty }})*

Gain a further extra melee attack. This can only be invoked directly
after a Fast Melee 1 that cost no stamina. As before, the roll that
tests the power is also the roll for the attack it grants.

Fast Melee 2 stacks with Fast Melee 1, and using the two together counts
as a single power use.

## Fast Ranged 1

*(stamina, ranged attack, difficulty {{ mechanics.fast_ranged_1_difficulty }})*

Gain an extra ranged attack. This works exactly like Fast Melee 1, but
for ranged attacks.
