Each weapon is listed with the statistics that feed [[hitting]] and
[[damage]]:

- **Accuracy** — a modifier to the attack roll.
- **Damage** — the weapon's damage rating, before the margin bonus and
  before [[armour]] reduction.
- **Size** — S, M or L.
- **Block value** — armour points subtracted from a blow when this
  weapon is used to block.

Blank entries below are weapons that are named in the draft but not yet
given numbers.

## Simple melee weapons

- **Short sword** — accuracy +1, damage 6, size S, block value 6.
- **Hand axe** — size S; other stats not yet set.
- **Dagger** — stats not yet set.
- **Staff** — stats not yet set.

## Martial melee weapons

- **Sword** — accuracy +1, damage 8, size M.
- **Battle axe** — damage 9, size M.
- **Two-handed sword** — damage 12, size L.
- **Great axe** — damage 12, size L.
