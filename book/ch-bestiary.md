title: Bestiary
kind: section
summary: Creatures, statted the same way characters are, with what they want and how they fight.
---

Everything in this chapter is built out of the same parts a character is
built from: six attributes, ranked skills, weapons and armour off the
equipment tables, and hit points in the same two pools. There is no
separate system for the other side of the table.

Each entry says what the creature is, how it fights, and what it wants —
which is usually the part that decides how the encounter goes.
