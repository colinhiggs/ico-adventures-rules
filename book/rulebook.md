*Icosahedron: a polyhedron with twenty faces.* Almost everything in this
game is settled by rolling one twenty-sided die, adding what your
character is good at, and seeing whether it was enough.

Read this in order the first time. After that, use the contents above —
each rule stands on its own well enough to be looked up in isolation,
and the in-game help shows the same text you are reading here.

There are no classes. A character is the sum of the **disciplines**
they have bought into, and a level is simply the next instalment of the
budget that buys them.
