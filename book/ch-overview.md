---

The Ico adventure system has a short list of goals, roughly in priority
order:

- **Fun.** It should be fun to play.
- **Storytelling.** The rules should push the group towards descriptive
  narration of the action rather than away from it.
- **Balance.** Two characters built for the same power level should each
  feel they contribute equally over a full adventure, and each should
  have something useful and non-trivial to do in most situations, and
  especially in combat.
- **Adventure.** The standard rules are tuned to make a life of high
  adventure less suicidal than the real thing would be: hit points run
  high relative to weapon damage, skills are impressive, powers are
  mighty.
- **Simplicity.** Not too many dice rolls, not too much arithmetic, not
  too many table lookups.
- **Realism.** Reality is modelled where doing so does not break one of
  the goals above.
